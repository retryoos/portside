What you are agreeing to

Purpose

This survey is part of an academic research project at the American College of Greece AI Lab, studying how maritime shipping professionals understand and use AI agents.

What it involves

Around 35 short questions: a few about you, then your views on AI agents in your work. It takes about nine minutes.

Voluntary participation

Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time before submitting by simply closing this page. Nothing is recorded until you press submit.

Anonymity and data

Your responses are anonymous. We do not collect your name, email, or any other identifying information. We record only your answers, the time of submission, and basic non-identifying technical information (your browser type). Data is used only in aggregate for research and may appear in academic publications.
